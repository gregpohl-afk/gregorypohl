 ORIGINAL CONCEPT

MY HOUSE

What if the competition came before the infrastructure?

3 PEOPLE.
1 REP.
3 BOARDS.
1 WORLD LADDER.

Most emerging sports build leagues, facilities and institutions first. My House starts with the thing people actually care about: who’s better? A globally comparable 1v1 performance becomes the competition itself; rank becomes the infrastructure.

Enough to show the idea. Not enough to hand over the machinery.

 METHOD

LIIR

Listen

Hear the internal story, including what is said, said carefully and what is avoided altogether.

Investigate

Test the story against external reality, evidence, incentives, human behavior and trends.

Identify

Name the constraint, the overlooked opportunity, or the piece of the system that is no longer serving a function.

Resolve
