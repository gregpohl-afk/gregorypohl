Is what I write when I want to stop your scroll. I imagine saying it in conversation, but it sounds awkward in my ears, and I realize that it’s performative, which I hate, but I can’t quite figure out how to get my point across without a list of recognizable logos and benchmarks. I’ve spent 25 years navigating the gray areas in hyper-regulated industries, winning awards, coaching other people to win awards, and learning to play with the narrative, all from inside the machine. I study. I study psychology, business, kinesics, economics, politics, philosophy, pop culture. I write movies. I write books. I enjoy the exploration, but I’m only really functioning at full capacity when I’m searching for the patterns. Everything at its root is a system of human dynamics and interconnectedness. Connections simply are. They exist whether or not anyone perceives them. I merely refuse to look away. Where some see two unrelated facts, I feel the tension between them — like a chord that hasn’t resolved. It itches at me until I find the note that makes it whole. I love crisis. I love the stakes. Had I redirected younger, I would have been an amazing hostage negotiator. I don’t panic. I find the thread. I’ve been offered substantial stakes and sums to stop seeking the horizon. But that leaves me unsettled. I’ve seen the damage an echo chamber creates, so I function beyond it. I have no ambition to outshine the master. I just want to be challenged and to meet that challenge. And I believe with every ounce of my being that I can help you get where you want to go.

I study human behavior, non-verbal cues, and micro-dynamics to see the motives and constraints hidden beneath the surface. Most organizations are blind here. I try not to be.

A brilliant answer to the wrong question is worthless. I challenge initial assumptions to isolate the real bottleneck. The solution usually solves itself.

Borders between industries are artificial. I practice relentless cross-domain synthesis — stealing models from biology, behavioral economics, and physics to bring non-obvious answers to complex problems.

WHY ME

BECAUSE I’M BUILT DIFFERENT.

I have the traditional corporate chops, but a creative’s mind. I’ve launched. I’ve scaled. I’ve pioneered. I’ve seen the game from all angles.

You see, most minds are triage machines. They discard. They file. They say, “This detail is irrelevant,” before they’ve earned the right to call it so. My particular affliction — and there are days I call it that — is an inability to discard. Everything stays lit.

When you spend years solving problems that have no precedent, pattern recognition stops being a skill and becomes a reflex. That’s usually where I find the thing everyone else is looking past.

 ORIGINAL CONCEPT

MY HOUSE

What if the competition came before the infrastructure?

3 PEOPLE.
1 REP.
3 BOARDS.
1 WORLD LADDER.

Most emerging sports build leagues, facilities and institutions first. My House starts with the thing people actually care about: who’s better? A globally comparable 1v1 performance becomes the competition itself; rank becomes the infrastructure.

Enough to show the idea. Not enough to hand over the machinery.
