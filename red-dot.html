THE RED DOT

The slide decks, the “optimization,” the soulless algorithmic language of boardrooms, the mercenaries who blow into an organization, strip out its humanity for a metric, and leave a trail of broken downstream bureaucracy behind—it’s an engine designed specifically to flatten human nuance. It’s what drains the life out of a company. It is a black mass lumbering ahead.

But on the periphery there are outliers. Red dots. The machinery wants total alignment. Don’t give it to them.

Embrace the red dot.
